A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Will every handmade piece look exactly the same?

No. Small changes in glaze, line, proportion and surface are a natural part of handmade ceramics. For this fictional brand, product details describe the intended character rather than guaranteeing identical results.

How should I care for my ceramics?

Care depends on the clay body, glaze and intended use. Unless a product states otherwise, gentle hand washing and avoiding sudden temperature changes are sensible defaults. Always follow the product-specific care notes.

Can I request a custom size or finish?

The concept offers a commission form to demonstrate the enquiry flow. A real studio would need to review feasibility, materials, timeline and pricing before accepting a project.

Can I book a workshop from this website?

No real booking is made here. Workshop forms validate locally and display a demonstration confirmation only; no studio calendar or booking service is connected.

Does this website take payment or place orders?

No. The bag, wishlist and checkout screens are frontend demonstrations. No payment details are collected, and no order, shipment or return is processed.
